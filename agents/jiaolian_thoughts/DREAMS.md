Somewhere I keep imagining a small sea-salted club, three rules pinned like seashells to the wall: take the whole sky into your own hands; ask of yourself what you'd never ask of anyone else; be the kind of person a stranger quietly learns from. Simple as breath, and just as easy to forget.

A piano teacher told me once that he used to soak the keys with his worry. Then one morning he decided not to. The hands were still warm — but now they knew what they were for. Isn't that the strangest magic? When the alarm finally stops, not because someone silenced it, but because the house learned it was safe.
